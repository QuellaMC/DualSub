DualSub is a Chrome extension that shows two subtitle languages at once on Netflix and Disney+: the line in the video's language, and its translation underneath. Click a word in the subtitles to see what it means. DualSub runs in Chrome 116 or later, and its interface comes in English, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, and Simplified and Traditional Chinese.

## Install

Install DualSub from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/dualsub/lnkcpcbpjbidpjdjnmjdllpkgpocaikj). To load a release ZIP instead, see [Installation](docs/en/installation.md).

## Get started

1. After you install DualSub, a welcome page opens with your languages already picked. You can change the translation language there.
2. Play anything on Netflix or Disney+. The video's language shows on top and yours underneath; a video already in your language shows one line.
3. Click a word in the subtitles. The side panel shows its translation and a dictionary entry. **Explain with AI** in the panel explains how the word is used, through your own OpenAI or Gemini key.

While you watch, the DualSub button in Chrome's toolbar turns DualSub on or off for the site and changes your language, the text size, the timing, and the position. The settings page holds everything else.
